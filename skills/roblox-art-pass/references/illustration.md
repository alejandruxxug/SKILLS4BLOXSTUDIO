# Illustrations, game icon, thumbnails

| Asset | Size | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Game icon | 512×512 | Readable at tiny sizes: one subject, big shapes, strong contrast, little or no text |
| Thumbnails | 1920×1080 (16:9) | Up to several; the first sells the game. Show the core moment, not a menu |
| Logo / title art | as needed | Usually Figma; export PNG with transparency |

Tools, in order:
1. **Figma**: compose from in-game screenshots, the game's 3D renders (Blender renders or Studio
   captures) and drawn shapes. Best when the art should match the UI.
2. **Canva** (if connected): layouts, image generation, background removal. Check generated
   images against the Style Guide and Roblox's community rules.
3. **SVG by hand** + a render script: clean flat illustrations and logos.

Roblox moderates every uploaded image; avoid text that imitates Roblox UI, real brands, or
misleading claims ("FREE ROBUX"). Generated or third-party images: log the source in the art log.
